# Ground Tile Pack

Three opaque 384×384 seamless ground tiles for LIGHT, BORDER, and DARK regions. Each tile is designed to repeat horizontally and vertically without a visible grid or border.

- ground-light.png: warm flagstones with light moss and grass.
- ground-border.png: cool slate-violet stones with more teal grass and moss.
- ground-dark.png: deep indigo stones with denser moss and low night lighting.

The source triptych was split into three square textures and its opposite edge bands were blended so left/right and top/bottom pixels match.
