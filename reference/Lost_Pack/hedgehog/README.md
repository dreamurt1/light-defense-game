# Hedgehog Resident Pack

Twenty-four transparent 512×512 sprites: 12 true-form and 12 lost-form, covering right-facing side, front, and rear views.

- Side, front, and rear views each have four slow walking frames.
- Feet rest at y=496; every sprite is centered at x=256. Front and rear views match the side view scale.
- True form: warm brown baby hedgehog with cream face and rounded cream-tipped spines.
- Lost form: matching dark plum (#1F1B2E) with lavender eyes #D6CCFF edged #A89CD9 in front and side views; no sclera, mouth, nose, or blush. Rear faces away and shows no eyes.
- No text, clothes, props, or ground shadow.
