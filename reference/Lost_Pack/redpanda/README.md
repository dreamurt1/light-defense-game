# Red Panda Resident Pack

Twenty-four transparent 512×512 sprites: 12 true-form and 12 lost-form, covering right-facing side, front, and rear views.

- Side, front, and rear views each have four quick zigzag walk frames.
- Feet rest at y=496; every sprite is centered at x=256. Front and rear views keep the head scale consistent with side views.
- True form: red-brown baby red panda with cream face markings and one large ringed tail.
- Lost form: matching dark plum (#1F1B2E) with lavender #D6CCFF eyes edged #A89CD9 in front and side views, no sclera, mouth, nose, or blush. Rear faces away and has no eyes.
- Lost rear-view frames are color-mapped from their matching true-form frames to preserve the exact single-tail silhouette and position.
- No text, clothes, props, or ground shadow.
